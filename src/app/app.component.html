VESSEL REGISTRATION SERVICES

Request for the Issuance of a Temporary Registration Certificate for Vessels and Maritime Units

This service enables individuals and companies to register a marine unit temporarily within the Sultanate of Oman and obtain a valid temporary registration certificate until all permanent registration requirements are fulfilled.

⚓ Ministry of Transport, Communications and Information Technology

Required Documents

You will need the following evidence before starting this service:

  • Previous registry / deletion or builder evidence
  • Classification and tonnage certificates
  • Ownership evidence
  • Radio / engine / technical evidence
  • Document supporting the intended marine activity

Service Request Steps

  1. Select User Type
  2. Select Commercial Registration
  3. Select Existing Ship or Enter New Ship Details
  4. Marine Unit Details

Service Terms

  • Vessel Owner / Authorized Representative of the Vessel Owner
  • Omani National or Legal Resident
